Nov 6, 2026

BC45 | NYC Premiere

December 13 at TV Eye in Ridgewood, Queens

BC45, a new short documentary about legendary recording engineer and producer Martin Bisi and his Brooklyn recording studio, premieres December 13 at TV Eye in Ridgewood, Queens.

Bisi has played an influential role in New York City's underground music scene for more than four decades, recording artists including Sonic Youth, Swans, and John Zorn.

I handled audio post-production for the film, continuing a longstanding collaboration with directors Sara Leavitt and Ryan Douglass.
